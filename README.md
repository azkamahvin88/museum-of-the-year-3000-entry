A make.fun entry for **Museum of the Year 3000**: one museum label — 121 characters — that a museum in the year 3000 puts next to an ordinary object from today. The program writes exactly that line to `/output/entry.txt`.

> CAPTCHA, c. 2025. A test in which humans proved they were human by identifying traffic lights, a task humans also failed.

The line was picked from 13 candidates scored against the judge brief's five criteria (on-subject, first-read laugh, year-3000 misreading, real label voice, freshness). The misreading IS the joke: the curator reports the CAPTCHA straight — a test where humans proved they were human — which is exactly what a CAPTCHA was. The absurdity is already in the truth: humanity had to audition for machines. The closer turns the test back on its makers: humans failed a test of being human. Deadpan, one breath, no explanation of its own joke, and a CAPTCHA is nowhere near the field's earbuds, house key, plastic fork or parking meter.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
